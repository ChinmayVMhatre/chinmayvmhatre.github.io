Research

Machine learning potentials for reactive systems

DTRA

Reactive events are rare, expensive to label, and poorly represented in equilibrium training data. I benchmarked an in-house active learning protocol against the published standard and cut compute hours in half at equal accuracy, then added a latent-space uncertainty metric so the loop can tell genuinely new configurations from ones it has already seen. With the trained potentials I ran over a hundred molecular dynamics simulations of catalytic kinetics at a scale DFT can't reach, which is how surface poisoning turned up as the root cause of performance loss. The protocol is now running on chemical threat neutralization in MOF-808.

Molecular transport in defective UiO-66

DTRA

Slow diffusion was the bottleneck on this material, so I engineered ordered point defects and got a hundredfold improvement in isopropanol diffusivity over a randomly defective structure. Pinning down what controls transport took self- and transport diffusivities across four concentrations, ten independent MSD analyses per point in LAMMPS, and GCMC adsorption in RASPA. Hydroxylated variants suppress transport as loading rises. On the reactivity side, CI-NEB calculations in VASP across four capping-group chemistries showed that the binding mechanism, not sterics, sets hydrolysis rates, and DFT-derived IR spectra gave the experimentalists vibrational fingerprints to check the modified frameworks against.

Reactive force fields for stainless steel corrosion

Naval Research Laboratory

Chloride-induced pitting starts at the atomic scale, and modeling it means having parameters that describe chromium oxide passive films breaking down. I'm developing and validating Cr/O/Cl ReaxFF parameters in LAMMPS for exactly that, which is a high-dimensional fitting problem against a small and precious reference set.

Thermophysical properties of fuel additives

Lubrizol

Three ZDDP molecules and their precursors had no experimental data at all, which makes synthesis prioritization guesswork. Gibbs ensemble Monte Carlo in Cassandra produced the first critical temperature and pressure estimates for them, validated within ten percent of the densities that were available. I also correlated conformational diversity with dipole moment magnitudes using dimensionality reduction, to connect structure back to the properties that matter.

Software

Electronic structure data pipeline for Gaussian output
Parses Gaussian output at scale to pull out bond order metrices, especially condensed-to-atoms density matrices, then writes to sharded HDF5 for easier parsing. Plotting includes generation of SOAP descriptors and UMAP embeddings. The script also parses ReaxFF and Pauling bond orders.
Kaggle competitions
Competition entry for the Regression of Molecular Properties challenge, using XGBoost to predict glass transition temperatures using SMILES representations, and GenAI project of generating molecular SMILES from mass spectroscopy data using Pytorch Transformer libraries.
NEB workflow tooling
ASE and LAMMPS scaffolding for reaction path calculations, including native partition-based NEB runs on SLURM clusters.
